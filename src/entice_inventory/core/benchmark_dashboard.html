Accounting & other KPIs

Origin–destination deliveries

Z + Y by producing origin and consuming destination, including domestic deliveries on the diagonal. Exports exclude the diagonal. In share mode, each final sector is compared with its baseline parent at the same origin.

Recorded trade target reconciliation

Recorded row-feasibility projection only, where its route-level audit is available. “Prior” is the already prepared target before this projection, not the original raw observation. Changes do not mean that aggregate world trade increased.

A common final demand shock

+1 M USD in one original sector and region. The final database distributes the same injection across its children and residual in proportion to saved X. Values below are total gross output responses, not GDP or welfare effects.

Stability & numerical diagnostics

A = Z / X by column; L = (I − A)⁻¹. Output multipliers are column sums of L. The 1-norm condition number is an estimate. Spectral radius below 1 is a productivity diagnostic under nonnegative A; tiny signed numerical values remain in the calculation. Output ≤1 USD is excluded from the linear system, with omitted gross flows reported.

Review queue

Open a sector to inspect the source of a flag. Share changes above 25 percentage points and common-shock multiplier changes above 0.25 are transparent screening rules, not economic acceptance thresholds. Negative labor/capital is flagged even if inherited.

How to read this benchmark

  1. Accounting checks are necessary. Final Z, Y, V and X are reaggregated to the exact pre-split baseline. EY and VY are compared unchanged. Numerical tolerances are 10⁻⁶ M USD + 10⁻⁸ × |reference| per cell; unchanged EY/VY are checked exactly, with EY units specific to each row.
  2. Inherited accounting is audited explicitly. X comes from X.parquet. Row discrepancies are Z·1 + Y·1 − X; column discrepancies are Zᵀ·1 + Vᵀ·1 − X. The expected final discrepancy equals the baseline parent's discrepancy times the final sector's X share. Discrepancies already present in the baseline are not silently balanced.
  3. Economic comparisons use the baseline parent. A new sector has no direct pre-split counterpart. Technology distance is the L1 sum of differences between input coefficients, after aggregating suppliers to original sectors and all origins. Value added and final demand retain their signs. Import share uses total intermediate inputs; export propensity and demand shares use X. Undefined ratios are shown as “—”, never as zero.
  4. Targets have already been harmonised. The selected output and trade support-table observations from each published stage are checked again. Unlisted trade routes remain unobserved; missing targets are not assumed zero. Agreement with these inputs is not independent empirical validation. The route audit covers only the recorded final trade projection, not the entire raw-to-final preparation chain.
  5. System changes are not conservation failures. Fine-sector linkages can change Leontief responses even if flows reaggregate exactly. Common shocks use a fixed output-share allocation and no optimisation. Parents for which the active detailed shares do not retain the full shock within 10⁻⁸ are excluded rather than renormalised.
  6. Environmental analysis is pending. E is provisional, so emissions, energy intensities and footprints are not benchmarked. No external/held-out data validation, order sensitivity, alternative model run or causal validation is claimed by this first version.
This report reads saved data only. It does not run a solver, modify the baseline, reconcile targets or rerun the MRIO model.
